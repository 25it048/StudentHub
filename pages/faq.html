Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register on StudentHub?

Create an account using the Register page by entering your personal and academic details.

Can I update my profile later?

Yes. You can edit your profile anytime from the Profile section.

How can I register for college events?

Open the Events page and click the Register button for your preferred event.

Is my data secure?

Yes. StudentHub stores student information securely.

Who can access the dashboard?

Only registered students with valid login credentials can access the dashboard.

How do I contact the administrator?

Visit the Contact page and submit the contact form.
